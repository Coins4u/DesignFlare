Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 5, 2026

DesignFlare ("we", "us", "our"), owned by Ayoub Esadik, operates the website at designflare.de and sells coded website templates, UI kits, and digital developer assets. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal information.

Information We Collect

  • Contact information (name, email, phone) submitted via checkout forms or contact pages
  • Order and billing details needed for bank transfer (we do not store payment card data)
  • Basic analytics (pages visited, device type, referral source) to improve our website
  • Email delivery metadata related to order confirmations, bank instructions, license credentials, and download links

How We Use Information

  • Process orders and deliver coded template ZIP files, license keys, and setup credentials
  • Send order confirmations and bank transfer instructions by email
  • Respond to support, licensing, and billing requests
  • Improve our templates, checkout flow, and website experience
  • Comply with legal, tax, and GDPR requirements

Bank Transfer & Data Retention

Payments are completed by bank transfer using details we email to you after your order is submitted. We do not store or process complete payment card details on our servers.

We retain order and contact data only as long as necessary for delivery, support, accounting, and legal compliance.

Third-Party Services

We may share limited data with service providers that help us operate our business, including email delivery services and hosting providers. These partners are required to handle data securely and only for authorized purposes.

Your Rights & Contact

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements.

For privacy questions, contact Ayoub Esadik at contact@designflare.de.
