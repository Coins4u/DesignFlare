About DesignFlare

DesignFlare is an independent digital studio founded by Ayoub Esadik, specializing in production-ready coded templates for developers, freelancers, and agencies. We build HTML/CSS landing pages, Next.js starter kits, React UI components, and email HTML templates so you can ship client projects and marketing sites in hours — not weeks.

Every package we sell is original source code, delivered as a licensed ZIP archive with setup instructions sent to your email after bank transfer payment is confirmed. We focus on clean code, commercial licensing clarity, and personal support so you can deploy with confidence.

What You Get

  • HTML, CSS, TSX, and API route source files
  • ZIP delivery via email after bank transfer is confirmed
  • Commercial license for client and business projects
  • Eight tiered packages from Essential Pack to Ultimate Collection
  • Direct support from Ayoub Esadik at contact@designflare.de

How It Works

Choose a package, submit your details at checkout, and we’ll email you personally with bank transfer details. Once payment is confirmed, your license credentials and ZIP download link arrive in your inbox — ready to drop into your Next.js, React, or static HTML project.
