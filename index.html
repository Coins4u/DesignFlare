Frequently Asked Questions

How do I access my email templates?

You'll receive a secure email immediately after purchase containing a download link to your ZIP archive with all source code files (HTML, CSS, TSX, API routes).

What license is included?

Every purchase includes a Commercial License. You can use these templates for your own business or your clients' businesses. Re-selling the templates themselves is not permitted.

What tech stack are the templates built with?

Low tiers use HTML/CSS and email HTML. Mid tiers include Next.js, React, and Tailwind CSS with API routes. High tiers include full React component libraries with white-label licensing.
